🔭 Astronomical Tools

From a first pair of binoculars to a radio dish spanning hundreds of meters — the right instrument transforms pinpoints of light into worlds, nebulae, and galaxies.

Telescopes collect photons (or radio waves) and concentrate them so our eyes — or cameras — can see what nature whispers across light-years. Each design trades portability, cost, and wavelength for a different view of the sky.

Choose a telescope type below to explore real examples, specs, and what each design does best.
