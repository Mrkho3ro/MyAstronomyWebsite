Schmidt-Cassegrain / Catadioptric

Schmidt-Cassegrain telescopes (SCTs) combine mirrors and a correcting lens plate in a compact tube. They offer long focal lengths in a portable package — great for planets, smaller deep-sky objects, and GoTo setups.
