Maksutov-Cassegrain Telescopes

Maksutov-Cassegrains use a thick meniscus corrector lens and spherical mirrors for a compact, rugged design. They excel at lunar and planetary views and are popular as portable spotting scopes and small GoTo systems.

Sky-Watcher Skymax 127

The Skymax 127 is a classic Maksutov-Cassegrain with a long f/12 focal ratio that excels at lunar and planetary detail. Its closed tube keeps dust out and reduces cooldown time, while the compact form factor makes it easy to set up on a tabletop or small equatorial mount.

127 mm f/12 | Tabletop / EQ mount | ~$400

Celestron NexStar 127SLT Mak

Combining Maksutov optics with Celestron's GoTo alt-az mount, the 127SLT is a travel-friendly system that finds and tracks targets automatically. The Mak design's inherent contrast makes Jupiter's cloud belts and the Moon's rilles stand out on steady nights.

127 mm f/12 | GoTo alt-az | ~$550

Orion Apex 102mm Maksutov

Designed as both an astronomy telescope and a high-power spotting scope, the Apex 102 slips into a daypack for dual-use observing. Its slow f/12.7 focal ratio delivers sharp, high-magnification views of the Moon's terminator and works equally well for distant terrestrial subjects.

102 mm f/12.7 | Spotting scope / visual | ~$300

Explore Scientific Maksutov 180

At 180 mm aperture and f/15, this is a serious planetary and double-star instrument that demands a sturdy mount. The large meniscus corrector and spherical mirrors produce diffraction-limited views when conditions allow — revealing fine detail on Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn that smaller scopes cannot match.

180 mm f/15 | Heavy-duty mount required | ~$1,800
