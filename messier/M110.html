M110 – Andromeda Satellite

  • Type: Elliptical Galaxy
  • Distance: ~2.69 million light-years
  • Constellation: Andromeda
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: A dwarf elliptical satellite galaxy of Andromeda, appearing as a faint oval near M31.
