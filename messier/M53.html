M53 – Globular Cluster

  • Type: Globular Cluster
  • Distance: ~58,000 light-years
  • Constellation: Coma Berenices
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: One of the most distant globular clusters in the catalog, with a bright condensed core.
