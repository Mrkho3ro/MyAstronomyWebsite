M102 – Spindle Galaxy

  • Type: Galaxy
  • Distance: ~50 million light-years
  • Constellation: Draco
  • Difficulty: Hard
  • Description: A lenticular galaxy seen nearly edge-on, appearing as a thin needle of light in telescopes.
