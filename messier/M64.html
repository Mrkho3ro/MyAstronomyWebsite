M64 – Black Eye Galaxy

  • Type: Galaxy
  • Distance: ~17 million light-years
  • Constellation: Coma Berenices
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: Named for the dark dust lane that blocks light from its bright nucleus, creating a black eye effect.
