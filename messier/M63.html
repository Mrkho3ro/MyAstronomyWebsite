M63 – Sunflower Galaxy

  • Type: Galaxy
  • Distance: ~37 million light-years
  • Constellation: Canes Venatici
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: A flocculent spiral galaxy with patchy spiral arms, resembling a sunflower in long exposures.
