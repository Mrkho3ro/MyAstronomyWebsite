M89 – Elliptical Galaxy

  • Type: Galaxy
  • Distance: ~50 million light-years
  • Constellation: Virgo
  • Difficulty: Hard
  • Description: A nearly perfectly spherical elliptical galaxy, one of the most round Messier objects.
