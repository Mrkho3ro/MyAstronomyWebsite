M77 – Cetus Galaxy

  • Type: Galaxy
  • Distance: ~47 million light-years
  • Constellation: Cetus
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: A bright Seyfert galaxy with an active nucleus, one of the largest Messier galaxies.
