M83 – Southern Pinwheel Galaxy

  • Type: Galaxy
  • Distance: ~15 million light-years
  • Constellation: Hydra
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: A barred spiral galaxy and one of the closest and brightest barred spirals in the sky.
