M24 – Sagittarius Star Cloud

  • Type: Star Cloud
  • Distance: ~10,000 light-years
  • Constellation: Sagittarius
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Description: Not a true cluster but a bright patch of the Milky Way's central star fields, visible to the naked eye.
