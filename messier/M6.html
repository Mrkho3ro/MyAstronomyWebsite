M6 – Butterfly Cluster

  • Type: Open Cluster
  • Distance: ~1,600 light-years
  • Constellation: Scorpius
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Description: An open cluster whose butterfly-wing shape is visible in binoculars near the tail of Scorpius.
