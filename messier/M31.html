M31 – Andromeda Galaxy

  • Type: Galaxy
  • Distance: ~2.5 million light-years
  • Constellation: Andromeda
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Description: The nearest major galaxy to the Milky Way and the most distant object visible to the naked eye.
