M57 – Ring Nebula

  • Type: Planetary Nebula
  • Distance: ~2,300 light-years
  • Constellation: Lyra
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: The most famous planetary nebula, appearing as a smoke ring or doughnut in telescopes.
