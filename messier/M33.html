M33 – Triangulum Galaxy

  • Type: Galaxy
  • Distance: ~2.73 million light-years
  • Constellation: Triangulum
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: The third-largest galaxy in the Local Group, a face-on spiral visible in binoculars under dark skies.
