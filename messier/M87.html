M87 – Virgo A

  • Type: Elliptical Galaxy
  • Distance: ~53 million light-years
  • Constellation: Virgo
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: A supergiant elliptical galaxy with a famous jet, home to the first photographed black hole.
