M11 – Wild Duck Cluster

  • Type: Open Cluster
  • Distance: ~6,200 light-years
  • Constellation: Scutum
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Description: One of the richest open clusters, with a distinctive V-shaped pattern resembling flying ducks.
