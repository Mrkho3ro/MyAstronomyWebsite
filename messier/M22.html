M22 – Sagittarius Cluster

  • Type: Globular Cluster
  • Distance: ~10,600 light-years
  • Constellation: Sagittarius
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Description: One of the brightest globular clusters in the sky, visible even to the naked eye under dark skies.
