M43 – De Mairan's Nebula

  • Type: Emission Nebula
  • Distance: ~1,600 light-years
  • Constellation: Orion
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Description: A separate section of the Orion Nebula complex, appearing as a comma-shaped glow north of M42.
