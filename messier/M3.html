M3 – Globular Cluster

  • Type: Globular Cluster
  • Distance: ~33,900 light-years
  • Constellation: Canes Venatici
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Description: A bright, rich globular cluster with hundreds of thousands of stars visible in small telescopes.
