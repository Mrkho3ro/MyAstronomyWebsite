M71 – Globular Cluster

  • Type: Globular Cluster
  • Distance: ~13,000 light-years
  • Constellation: Sagittarius
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Description: Once thought to be an open cluster, this loose globular is easily resolved into stars.
