M76 – Little Dumbbell Nebula

  • Type: Planetary Nebula
  • Distance: ~2,500 light-years
  • Constellation: Perseus
  • Difficulty: Hard
  • Description: A faint bipolar planetary nebula resembling a miniature version of the Dumbbell Nebula.
