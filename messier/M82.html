M82 – Cigar Galaxy

  • Type: Galaxy
  • Distance: ~12 million light-years
  • Constellation: Ursa Major
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Description: A starburst galaxy edge-on to Earth, famous for its irregular red hydrogen outflows.
