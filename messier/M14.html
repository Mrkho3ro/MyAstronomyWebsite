M14 – Globular Cluster

  • Type: Globular Cluster
  • Distance: ~30,300 light-years
  • Constellation: Ophiuchus
  • Difficulty: Hard
  • Description: A moderately bright globular cluster with a compressed core, best viewed with larger apertures.
