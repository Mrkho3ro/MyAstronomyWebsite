How Far Is Light?

Light from the Sun takes minutes to hours to reach each planet. These one-way travel times make the scale of the Solar System feel tangible — radio signals to spacecraft face the same delay.

  • Mercury~3.2 min
  • Venus~6 min
  • Earth~8.3 min
  • Mars~12.7 min
  • Jupiter~43 min
  • Saturn~1.3 hr
  • Uranus~2.7 hr
  • Neptune~4.1 hr

Between the Planets

Two vast rings of leftover material frame the giant planets — visible as dusty belts in the 3D explorer above.

Asteroid Belt

~2.2–3.3 AU · between Mars & Jupiter

Millions of rocky fragments orbit here — leftovers that never formed a planet, stirred by Jupiter’s gravity. Ceres, a dwarf planet, is the largest resident.

Kuiper Belt

~30–50 AU · beyond Neptune

A cold reservoir of icy bodies and dwarf planets including Pluto. Short-period comets are thought to originate from this distant frontier.
