Earth

Our home planet, Earth is the only known place with life. It has liquid water, a breathable atmosphere, and diverse ecosystems.

  • Type: Terrestrial (rocky)
  • Distance from Sun: ~150 million km (1 AU)
  • Orbital period: 365.25 days
  • Moons: 1 (the Moon)
  • Atmosphere: 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen

Interesting Facts

  • About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by oceans.
  • Earth's magnetic field protects life from harmful solar radiation.
  • Plate tectonics recycle the crust and help regulate climate over geologic time.
  • Earth is the densest planet in the Solar System.
  • From space, Earth appears as a pale blue dot—a fragile oasis in the cosmos.
