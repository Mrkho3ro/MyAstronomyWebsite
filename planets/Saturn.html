Saturn

Saturn is best known for its stunning ring system. It's a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium.

  • Type: Gas giant
  • Distance from Sun: ~1.4 billion km
  • Orbital period: ~29 Earth years
  • Known moons: 140+ (including Titan and Enceladus)
  • Density: Less dense than water—it would float in a giant bathtub

Interesting Facts

  • Saturn's rings are made of countless ice and rock particles, some as small as grains of sand.
  • Titan has lakes of liquid methane and a thick nitrogen atmosphere.
  • Enceladus shoots geysers of water ice from its south pole into space.
  • The Cassini spacecraft orbited Saturn for 13 years, revolutionizing our understanding.
  • Hexagonal jet streams swirl around Saturn's north pole—a unique atmospheric feature.
