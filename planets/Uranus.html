Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant that rotates on its side, making it unique. It has a pale blue color due to methane in its atmosphere.

  • Type: Ice giant
  • Distance from Sun: ~2.9 billion km
  • Orbital period: ~84 Earth years
  • Known moons: 27 (including Miranda and Titania)
  • Axial tilt: ~98°—it rolls around the Sun on its side

Interesting Facts

  • Uranus was the first planet discovered with a telescope (William Herschel, 1781).
  • Its extreme tilt may result from a massive collision early in its history.
  • Uranus has faint, dark rings discovered in 1977.
  • It is the coldest planetary atmosphere in the Solar System, down to −224 °C.
  • Uranus and Neptune are often grouped as "ice giants" distinct from Jupiter and Saturn.
