Mercury

The closest planet to the Sun, Mercury is a small, rocky world with extreme temperatures, ranging from scorching hot days to freezing cold nights.

  • Type: Terrestrial (rocky)
  • Distance from Sun: ~58 million km
  • Orbital period: 88 Earth days
  • Day length: 59 Earth days
  • Moons: 0

Interesting Facts

  • Mercury has almost no atmosphere, so heat escapes quickly at night.
  • Its surface is covered in craters, similar to Earth's Moon.
  • Despite being closest to the Sun, Venus is hotter overall.
  • Mercury's iron core makes up about 75% of its radius.
  • NASA's MESSENGER and Mariner missions mapped most of its surface.
