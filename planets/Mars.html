Mars

Known as the Red Planet, Mars has a dusty surface and signs of ancient water. Scientists study it closely for clues about past life.

  • Type: Terrestrial (rocky)
  • Distance from Sun: ~228 million km
  • Orbital period: 687 Earth days
  • Moons: 2 (Phobos and Deimos)
  • Home to Olympus Mons—the tallest volcano in the Solar System

Interesting Facts

  • Mars gets its red color from iron oxide (rust) dust on its surface.
  • River valleys and lake beds suggest Mars once had flowing liquid water.
  • Dust storms can engulf the entire planet for weeks.
  • NASA rovers including Perseverance actively search for signs of ancient microbial life.
  • A day on Mars (a "sol") is about 24 hours and 37 minutes.
