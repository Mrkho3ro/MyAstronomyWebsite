Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System, a gas giant famous for its Great Red Spot—a massive, long-lasting storm.

  • Type: Gas giant
  • Distance from Sun: ~779 million km
  • Orbital period: ~12 Earth years
  • Known moons: 95+ (including Ganymede, Europa, Io, Callisto)
  • Mass: More than twice all other planets combined

Interesting Facts

  • The Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth that has raged for centuries.
  • Jupiter's strong gravity helps shield inner planets by deflecting asteroids and comets.
  • Europa, one of its moons, may harbor a subsurface ocean beneath ice.
  • Jupiter has faint rings, discovered by Voyager 1 in 1979.
  • Its rapid rotation (about 10 hours) creates dramatic cloud bands and jet streams.
