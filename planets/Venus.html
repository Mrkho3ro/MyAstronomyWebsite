Venus

Venus is similar in size to Earth but covered in thick, toxic clouds. It's the hottest planet in the Solar System due to its powerful greenhouse effect.

  • Type: Terrestrial (rocky)
  • Distance from Sun: ~108 million km
  • Orbital period: 225 Earth days
  • Surface temperature: ~465 °C (870 °F)
  • Moons: 0

Interesting Facts

  • Venus rotates backward compared to most planets (retrograde rotation).
  • Its thick atmosphere is mostly carbon dioxide with clouds of sulfuric acid.
  • Venus is often called Earth's "sister planet" because of similar size and mass.
  • A day on Venus (243 Earth days) is longer than its year.
  • Surface pressure is about 90 times that of Earth—like being 900 m underwater.
