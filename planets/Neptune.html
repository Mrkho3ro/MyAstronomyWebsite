Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun and is known for its deep blue color and extremely strong winds—the fastest in the Solar System.

  • Type: Ice giant
  • Distance from Sun: ~4.5 billion km
  • Orbital period: ~165 Earth years
  • Known moons: 16 (including Triton)
  • Wind speeds: Up to 2,100 km/h (1,300 mph)

Interesting Facts

  • Neptune was predicted mathematically before it was seen through a telescope (1846).
  • Its moon Triton orbits backward and is likely a captured Kuiper Belt object.
  • Neptune radiates more heat than it receives from the Sun—its interior is still cooling.
  • The Great Dark Spot was a storm similar to Jupiter's Great Red Spot, observed by Voyager 2.
  • One Neptunian year lasts so long that it has not completed a full orbit since its discovery.
